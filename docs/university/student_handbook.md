# Northbridge University
## Student Handbook

### 1. Welcome to Northbridge University

The Northbridge University Student Handbook provides general information about student responsibilities, academic procedures, campus services, and university expectations.

Students should use this handbook together with the current academic calendar and programme-specific information.

University procedures and requirements may change, so students should check official announcements for the most current information.

---

### 2. Student Responsibilities

Students are responsible for managing their academic and administrative responsibilities throughout their studies.

Students should:

- Attend required academic activities.
- Complete module registration on time.
- Monitor academic deadlines.
- Complete assessments according to module requirements.
- Follow examination procedures.
- Maintain academic integrity.
- Keep their student information up to date.
- Check official university announcements.
- Seek assistance when they encounter academic difficulties.

---

### 3. Student Identification

Students should use their university student identification when accessing university services where identification is required.

Students are responsible for keeping their identification information secure.

Students should report problems with their student identification to the appropriate university office.

---

### 4. Academic Communication

Northbridge University may communicate important academic information through official channels.

These may include:

- The student information system.
- University email.
- Official university announcements.
- The university website.
- Academic support staff.

Students should regularly check official communication channels.

Students are responsible for reviewing important announcements that apply to their programme.

---

### 5. Academic Support

Students who need help with academic matters may contact the Academic Support Office.

Support may include guidance about:

- Module registration.
- Programme requirements.
- Academic progression.
- Module selection.
- Module changes.
- Assessment concerns.
- Examination information.
- Study planning.

Students are encouraged to seek assistance before a deadline whenever possible.

---

### 6. Library Services

The university library provides students with access to academic resources and study facilities.

Students should follow library rules regarding:

- Borrowing materials.
- Returning materials.
- Use of study spaces.
- Access to electronic resources.
- Appropriate use of library facilities.

Students who need help finding academic resources may contact library staff.

---

### 7. Information Technology Services

Students may use university information technology services for academic activities.

IT services may include:

- Student accounts.
- Campus internet access.
- Computer laboratories.
- Learning platforms.
- Technical support.

Students should protect their account credentials and should not share passwords with other people.

Technical problems should be reported to the university IT Support Office.

---

### 8. Student Conduct

Students are expected to behave respectfully toward other students, staff, and visitors.

Students should:

- Respect university facilities.
- Follow university rules.
- Communicate respectfully.
- Avoid disruptive behaviour.
- Follow academic integrity requirements.
- Follow examination rules.

Serious conduct concerns may be referred to the appropriate university authority.

---

### 9. Academic Integrity

Students must complete academic work honestly.

Academic misconduct may include:

- Plagiarism.
- Unauthorized collaboration.
- Cheating.
- Submitting another person's work as one's own.
- Using unauthorized materials during an examination.

Students should ask their lecturer or academic support staff when they are unsure whether an academic activity is permitted.

---

### 10. Health, Safety and Emergencies

Students should follow university health and safety instructions while on campus.

Emergency situations should be reported through the university's designated emergency procedures.

Students should follow instructions provided by university staff during emergencies.

---

### 11. Student Complaints and Appeals

Students who wish to raise an academic or administrative concern should follow the appropriate university complaint or appeal procedure.

Students should provide accurate information and submit requests within the applicable deadline.

Students should retain copies of relevant documents and correspondence.

---

### 12. Updating Student Information

Students should ensure that important personal and academic information in the student information system is accurate.

Students should report incorrect information to the appropriate university office.

---

### 13. Student Responsibilities Summary

Students should:

1. Monitor official university communication.
2. Complete academic procedures before deadlines.
3. Attend required academic activities.
4. Maintain academic integrity.
5. Protect university accounts and resources.
6. Follow campus rules.
7. Seek academic support when necessary.
8. Keep important records and documents.
9. Check current university procedures before submitting requests.

---

### 14. Important Reminder

The Student Handbook provides general guidance. Programme-specific requirements and current university procedures take precedence where applicable.

Students should consult official university information and the relevant academic office when they need clarification.
