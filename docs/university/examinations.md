# Northbridge University
## Examination and Assessment Guide

### 1. Overview

Examinations are an important part of academic assessment at Northbridge University.

Students are responsible for understanding the examination requirements for their registered modules and following the university's examination procedures.

---

### 2. Examination Schedule

The university publishes examination schedules through official communication channels.

Students should check the examination schedule when it becomes available.

The schedule may include:

- Examination date.
- Examination time.
- Examination venue.
- Module information.
- Examination instructions.

Students should verify their examination information before the examination period begins.

---

### 3. Preparing for an Examination

Students should prepare for examinations by reviewing:

- Module learning materials.
- Assessment requirements.
- Examination instructions.
- Relevant revision materials.
- The published examination schedule.

Students should plan sufficient preparation time rather than relying on last-minute study.

---

### 4. Examination Attendance

Students are expected to attend scheduled examinations for their registered modules.

Students should arrive at the examination venue according to the instructions provided by the university.

Students who know that they cannot attend an examination should contact the appropriate academic office as soon as possible.

---

### 5. Examination Conduct

Students must follow examination instructions provided by the university and examination staff.

During an examination, students should:

- Follow instructions from invigilators.
- Use only permitted materials.
- Avoid unauthorized communication.
- Remain in the examination area unless permission is provided.
- Follow all examination procedures.

Students must not use unauthorized resources or communicate with other students during an examination.

---

### 6. Permitted Materials

The materials permitted during an examination depend on the specific examination.

Students should check the examination instructions before bringing materials into the examination room.

Students should not assume that a material is permitted unless the examination instructions explicitly allow it.

---

### 7. Electronic Devices

Electronic devices may be restricted during examinations.

Students should follow the instructions provided for each examination regarding:

- Mobile phones.
- Smart watches.
- Tablets.
- Laptops.
- Calculators.
- Other electronic devices.

Unauthorized use of an electronic device may be treated as an examination conduct issue.

---

### 8. Examination Problems

Students who experience a problem during an examination should inform the invigilator as soon as possible.

Examples of problems include:

- A missing examination paper.
- Technical problems where technology is being used.
- Health or safety concerns.
- Problems with the examination environment.

Students should report problems during the examination whenever possible.

---

### 9. Absence from an Examination

Students who cannot attend an examination should follow the university's applicable absence procedure.

Students may be required to provide supporting information or documentation depending on the circumstances.

Students should contact the relevant academic office promptly rather than waiting until after the examination period.

---

### 10. Assessment Submission

Some modules may use coursework, projects, presentations, or other forms of assessment instead of or in addition to examinations.

Students are responsible for checking the assessment requirements for each registered module.

Assessment submissions must be completed according to the instructions and deadlines provided by the module.

---

### 11. Academic Integrity During Examinations

Students must maintain academic integrity during examinations.

Students must not:

- Copy another student's work.
- Communicate with other students without permission.
- Use unauthorized materials.
- Receive unauthorized assistance.
- Use another person's identity or work.

Suspected examination misconduct may be referred for further review under university procedures.

---

### 12. Examination Results

Examination results are published through the university's designated student information system when they become available.

Students should review their results and check whether any further academic action is required.

Students with questions about their results should contact the relevant academic office.

---

### 13. Reviewing Results

Students should review their examination and assessment results after publication.

Students should check:

- Module results.
- Overall academic progress.
- Remaining programme requirements.
- Any academic conditions or requirements.

Students who believe there may be an error should follow the applicable university review or appeal procedure.

---

### 14. Appeals and Reviews

Students who wish to question an academic result must follow the university's applicable review or appeal procedure.

Students should:

1. Review the relevant assessment information.
2. Check the applicable deadline.
3. Gather supporting information where required.
4. Submit the appropriate request.
5. Retain a copy of the submitted request.

Students should not assume that a review will automatically change an academic result.

---

### 15. Examination Support

Students who have questions about examination procedures should contact the relevant academic office.

Students may seek clarification about:

- Examination schedules.
- Examination rules.
- Assessment requirements.
- Examination absence procedures.
- Results.
- Review and appeal procedures.

---

### 16. Examination Summary

Students should:

1. Check the examination schedule.
2. Review examination instructions.
3. Prepare appropriately.
4. Arrive according to the examination requirements.
5. Follow invigilator instructions.
6. Use only permitted materials.
7. Report examination problems promptly.
8. Check results when they are published.
9. Follow the appropriate review or appeal procedure when necessary.
