# Northbridge University
## Campus Services

### 1. Overview

Northbridge University provides a range of campus services to support students during their studies.

These services include library facilities, information technology support, student administration, study spaces, and general student assistance.

---

### 2. University Library

The university library provides resources and facilities for academic study.

Services may include:

- Printed academic resources.
- Electronic resources.
- Study spaces.
- Computer facilities.
- Research assistance.
- Borrowing services.

Students should follow library rules when using library resources and facilities.

---

### 3. Library Borrowing

Students who borrow library materials are responsible for returning them according to the applicable borrowing period.

Students should check their library account for information about borrowed materials and due dates.

Students should contact library staff if they have questions about borrowing or returning resources.

---

### 4. Information Technology Support

The IT Support Office assists students with university technology services.

Support may include:

- Student accounts.
- University email.
- Learning platforms.
- Campus internet.
- Computer laboratories.
- Access to university systems.

Students should report technical problems through the appropriate IT support channel.

---

### 5. Student Accounts

Students use university accounts to access selected academic and administrative services.

Students are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure.

Students should:

- Use strong passwords.
- Avoid sharing passwords.
- Sign out of shared computers.
- Report suspected account security problems.

---

### 6. Learning Platforms

Some modules may use university learning platforms to provide:

- Learning materials.
- Announcements.
- Assessment information.
- Submission areas.
- Module communication.

Students should regularly check the learning platform for information related to their registered modules.

---

### 7. Student Administration

Student Administration supports administrative processes relating to student records and university procedures.

Students may contact Student Administration about matters such as:

- Student records.
- Official documents.
- Administrative requests.
- Student status information.

Students should provide accurate information when submitting administrative requests.

---

### 8. Study Spaces

The university provides designated spaces for individual and group study.

Students using study spaces should:

- Keep noise at an appropriate level.
- Respect other students.
- Keep study areas clean.
- Follow facility rules.
- Leave shared resources available for other students.

---

### 9. Student Support

Students may seek general assistance when they are unsure which university service can address their question.

Students may be directed to:

- Academic Support.
- Student Administration.
- IT Support.
- Library Services.
- Programme staff.
- Other relevant university offices.

---

### 10. Accessibility Support

Students who require assistance accessing university facilities or academic services should contact the appropriate university support office.

Students should communicate their requirements early where possible so that appropriate arrangements can be considered.

---

### 11. Campus Facilities

Campus facilities may include:

- Lecture rooms.
- Laboratories.
- Libraries.
- Computer rooms.
- Study spaces.
- Student activity areas.
- Administrative offices.

Students are expected to use university facilities responsibly.

---

### 12. Reporting Problems

Students should report problems with university facilities or services to the relevant office.

Examples include:

- IT system problems.
- Facility maintenance issues.
- Problems accessing academic services.
- Problems with university equipment.

Students should provide enough information for staff to understand the issue.

---

### 13. Service Information

Operating arrangements for university services may vary during holidays, examination periods, or other academic periods.

Students should check current university announcements for service availability and changes.

---

### 14. Summary

Students can access several university services during their studies.

The main services include:

- Library Services.
- IT Support.
- Student Administration.
- Academic Support.
- Study facilities.
- General student assistance.

Students should contact the relevant service when they need assistance and check current official information for service arrangements.
