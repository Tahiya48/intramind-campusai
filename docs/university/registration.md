# Northbridge University
## Module Registration Policy

### 1. Overview

Module registration is the process through which students select the modules they intend to study during an academic semester.

At Northbridge University, students are responsible for reviewing their programme requirements and completing module registration within the designated registration period.

Students should complete registration before the published registration deadline. Failure to complete registration on time may affect a student's ability to obtain a place in selected modules.

---

### 2. Online Module Registration

Northbridge University uses an Online Module Registration (OMR) system for module selection.

Through the OMR system, students can:

- View modules available for their programme.
- Select modules for the upcoming semester.
- Review their selected modules.
- Check module prerequisites.
- Confirm their registration.
- Make permitted changes during the module adjustment period.

Students should carefully review all information displayed in the registration system before submitting their final registration.

---

### 3. Registration Period

Module registration takes place during a designated registration period before the beginning of each academic semester.

The registration period is announced through the university's academic calendar and student information system.

Students should complete their registration as early as possible rather than waiting until the final day.

Registration deadlines may vary between academic semesters. Students are responsible for checking the applicable deadline for their academic period.

---

### 4. Student Eligibility

Students must be academically eligible to register for their selected modules.

Before registering, students should check:

- Their programme requirements.
- Their current academic level.
- Required modules for the semester.
- Module prerequisites.
- Any restrictions associated with particular modules.
- Their study plan.

Students should not register for a module if they have not satisfied a required prerequisite unless the university has explicitly granted an exception.

---

### 5. Reviewing Programme Requirements

Before submitting final registration, students should compare their selected modules with their programme requirements.

Students should make sure that:

- Required modules have been included.
- Selected modules contribute toward their programme requirements.
- Prerequisites have been satisfied.
- The selected modules are appropriate for their current study plan.
- The total module selection is consistent with the student's academic plan.

Students who are unsure whether a module is appropriate should contact the Academic Support Office before finalising registration.

---

### 6. Reviewing the Final Registration

Students should carefully review their registration before submitting it.

The final review should include:

1. Checking the names of all selected modules.
2. Confirming that required modules have been selected.
3. Checking module prerequisites.
4. Confirming that unwanted modules have not been selected.
5. Reviewing the planned timetable when available.
6. Confirming that the registration meets programme requirements.
7. Checking for any registration warnings or restrictions.
8. Submitting the final registration before the deadline.

Once registration has been submitted, students should keep a record of their registered modules.

---

### 7. Adding a Module

Students who need to add a module should do so through the university's approved module adjustment process.

A module may only be added when:

- The module is available to the student's programme.
- The student satisfies the relevant prerequisites.
- The module is still open for registration.
- The request is made within the permitted adjustment period.

Students should review their updated module selection after adding a module.

---

### 8. Dropping a Module

Students who no longer intend to take a registered module may request to drop it during the permitted module adjustment period.

Before dropping a module, students should consider whether the change affects:

- Programme requirements.
- Prerequisites for future modules.
- Their academic study plan.
- Their total registered modules.

Students who are unsure about the consequences of dropping a module should seek academic advice before submitting the request.

---

### 9. Withdrawing from a Module

Module withdrawal is different from simply changing a module selection during the registration period.

Students who need to withdraw from a module must follow the university's module withdrawal procedure.

The applicable procedure and deadline depend on the academic period and the student's circumstances.

Students should consult the Academic Support Office if they are unsure whether they should drop or withdraw from a module.

---

### 10. Late Module Registration

Students who fail to complete registration during the normal registration period may need to follow the university's late registration procedure.

Late registration requests may be subject to additional requirements and approval.

Students should contact the Academic Support Office as soon as possible if they have missed the normal registration deadline.

Students should not assume that late registration will automatically be approved.

---

### 11. Registration Errors

If a student notices an error in their module registration, they should report the issue to the Academic Support Office.

Examples of registration issues include:

- A required module is missing.
- An incorrect module has been selected.
- A prerequisite warning appears unexpectedly.
- A registered module does not appear in the student's timetable.
- A module change has not been reflected in the registration system.

Students should provide relevant registration information when requesting assistance.

---

### 12. Registration Support

The Academic Support Office provides assistance with questions relating to module registration.

Students may contact the office when they need help with:

- Understanding programme requirements.
- Checking module prerequisites.
- Completing online registration.
- Adding or dropping modules.
- Understanding module withdrawal procedures.
- Resolving registration errors.
- Understanding registration deadlines.

Students should seek assistance before the registration deadline whenever possible.

---

### 13. Important Registration Deadlines

Registration deadlines are determined for each academic period.

Students should check the official Northbridge University academic calendar for the current registration dates.

Important dates may include:

- Opening of the module registration period.
- Closing of the normal registration period.
- Late registration period.
- Module add/drop deadline.
- Module withdrawal deadline.

Students are responsible for checking the deadlines that apply to their programme and academic period.

---

### 14. Student Responsibilities

Students are responsible for ensuring that their module registration is accurate and completed on time.

Students should:

- Monitor registration announcements.
- Review their programme requirements.
- Check module prerequisites.
- Complete registration within the designated period.
- Review their final module selection.
- Keep a record of their registered modules.
- Seek academic assistance when necessary.
- Check for changes to registration deadlines.

The university recommends that students do not wait until the final day of registration to resolve registration issues.

---

### 15. Summary

The module registration process at Northbridge University can be summarised as follows:

1. Check the academic calendar for registration dates.
2. Review programme and module requirements.
3. Check prerequisites and eligibility.
4. Select appropriate modules through the OMR system.
5. Review the selected modules carefully.
6. Confirm that the selection fits the student's study plan.
7. Submit the final registration before the deadline.
8. Follow the appropriate procedure if modules need to be added, dropped, or withdrawn.
9. Contact the Academic Support Office if assistance is required.

Students should always use the current academic calendar and university procedures when determining the deadlines and requirements that apply to their registration.
