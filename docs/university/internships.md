# Northbridge University
## Internship and Work Placement Guide

### 1. Internship Overview

An internship is a supervised period of practical work experience that allows students to apply knowledge and skills gained during their studies.

Internships may be required, recommended, or optional depending on the student's programme.

Students should check their programme requirements before planning an internship.

---

### 2. Internship Eligibility

Students must meet the eligibility requirements specified by their programme before beginning an internship.

Eligibility may depend on:

- Programme requirements.
- Academic standing.
- Completion of prerequisite modules.
- Required academic credits.
- Availability of an approved internship placement.

Students should confirm their eligibility with the Academic Support Office or programme coordinator.

---

### 3. Finding an Internship

Students may identify internship opportunities through:

- University career services.
- Employer recruitment events.
- University internship listings.
- Direct applications to organizations.
- Professional networking.

Students are responsible for ensuring that a proposed internship meets university requirements before accepting the placement.

---

### 4. Internship Approval

Students should obtain university approval before beginning an internship that is intended to satisfy a programme requirement.

The proposed internship may need to be reviewed by the programme coordinator.

Students should provide information about:

- The organization.
- The internship role.
- The expected duration.
- The responsibilities involved.
- The proposed start and end dates.
- The workplace supervisor.

Students should not assume that an internship will count toward their programme without the required approval.

---

### 5. Internship Duration

The required internship duration depends on the student's programme.

Students should check their programme-specific requirements before accepting a placement.

The university may require students to complete a minimum number of working hours or weeks.

---

### 6. Required Documents

Students may be required to submit documentation before an internship begins.

Documents may include:

- Internship offer letter.
- Internship description.
- Employer information.
- Internship approval form.
- Student information.
- Supervisor information.
- Required university agreements.

Students should submit documents by the applicable deadline.

---

### 7. Internship Responsibilities

Students participating in an internship are expected to behave professionally.

Students should:

- Attend the internship according to the agreed schedule.
- Complete assigned responsibilities.
- Communicate professionally.
- Follow workplace policies.
- Follow university internship requirements.
- Maintain confidentiality where required.
- Communicate with their university supervisor when necessary.

---

### 8. Workplace Supervisor

The workplace supervisor provides guidance during the internship.

The supervisor may be responsible for:

- Assigning tasks.
- Providing workplace guidance.
- Monitoring student progress.
- Providing feedback.
- Completing required evaluation forms.

Students should communicate with their supervisor when they need clarification about assigned work.

---

### 9. University Supervisor

Where applicable, the university may assign an academic or internship supervisor.

The university supervisor may:

- Monitor internship progress.
- Review required documentation.
- Communicate with the student.
- Provide academic guidance.
- Review internship assessment requirements.

---

### 10. Internship Assessment

Students may be required to complete an assessment as part of their internship.

Assessment requirements may include:

- Internship reports.
- Supervisor evaluations.
- Presentations.
- Reflective assignments.
- Logbooks or activity records.

Students should check the assessment requirements for their programme.

---

### 11. Internship Report

Where an internship report is required, students should follow the format and submission requirements provided by their programme.

A report may describe:

- The organization.
- The student's role.
- Tasks completed.
- Skills developed.
- Challenges encountered.
- Lessons learned.
- Reflection on the internship experience.

Students should submit the report by the published deadline.

---

### 12. Problems During an Internship

Students who experience significant problems during an internship should contact their university internship coordinator or Academic Support Office.

Examples may include:

- Major changes to internship responsibilities.
- Problems with the workplace environment.
- Changes to the internship duration.
- Problems with supervision.
- Inability to continue the placement.

Students should communicate problems as early as possible.

---

### 13. Changing an Internship

Students should not change or terminate an approved internship without consulting the appropriate university office when the internship is being used to satisfy a programme requirement.

A replacement placement may require new approval.

---

### 14. Internship Completion

Students should complete all required internship activities and submit the required documentation before the applicable deadline.

Students should confirm that all required assessments and supervisor forms have been submitted.

---

### 15. Internship Summary

Students planning an internship should:

1. Check programme requirements.
2. Confirm eligibility.
3. Find a suitable placement.
4. Obtain university approval.
5. Submit required documentation.
6. Complete the internship professionally.
7. Maintain communication with the university where required.
8. Complete required assessments.
9. Submit the internship report by the deadline.

Students should contact the programme coordinator when they are uncertain about an internship requirement.
