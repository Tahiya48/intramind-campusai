# Northbridge University
## Academic Support Services

### 1. Overview

The Academic Support Office provides guidance to students who need assistance with academic procedures, programme requirements, study planning, and academic progress.

Students are encouraged to seek support early when they are uncertain about an academic requirement.

---

### 2. Module Registration Support

Students can contact Academic Support for help with:

- Module registration.
- Module selection.
- Programme requirements.
- Module prerequisites.
- Registration errors.
- Adding or dropping modules.
- Module withdrawal procedures.
- Registration deadlines.

Students should seek assistance before the applicable deadline whenever possible.

---

### 3. Study Planning

Academic Support can help students review their study plans.

Students may seek guidance about:

- Required modules.
- Elective modules.
- Remaining programme requirements.
- Module sequencing.
- Prerequisites.
- Expected programme progression.

Students remain responsible for confirming their final module selection.

---

### 4. Academic Progress

Students who are concerned about their academic performance or progression can request academic guidance.

Students may discuss:

- Completed modules.
- Outstanding modules.
- Academic results.
- Study plans.
- Programme requirements.
- Difficulties affecting academic progress.

Students should seek support before academic difficulties become more difficult to address.

---

### 5. Assessment Support

Students who experience difficulties with assessments should contact the appropriate academic office.

Support may involve explaining:

- Assessment procedures.
- Submission requirements.
- Assessment deadlines.
- Available academic procedures.
- Appropriate sources of assistance.

Students should contact the university as soon as possible when an issue may affect their ability to complete an assessment.

---

### 6. Examination Support

Academic Support can provide general guidance about examination procedures.

Students may ask about:

- Examination schedules.
- Examination procedures.
- Examination absence.
- Examination conduct.
- Results.
- Review and appeal procedures.

Students should follow the specific instructions provided for their examinations.

---

### 7. Internship Support

Students may contact Academic Support or the relevant programme coordinator for guidance about internship requirements.

Support may include:

- Internship eligibility.
- Internship approval.
- Required documentation.
- Internship duration.
- Internship assessment.
- Internship reports.
- Problems during an internship.

Students should confirm that an internship satisfies programme requirements before relying on it for academic credit.

---

### 8. Study Deferment Support

Students considering a temporary interruption of their studies can request information about study deferment procedures.

Students may need to review:

- Eligibility.
- Application deadlines.
- Required documents.
- Deferment duration.
- Effects on academic progression.

Students should obtain guidance before submitting a deferment request if they are uncertain about the process.

---

### 9. Programme Withdrawal Support

Students considering permanent withdrawal from their programme should seek appropriate guidance before submitting a withdrawal request.

Academic Support can help students understand the relevant procedure and identify questions that should be considered before making a final decision.

---

### 10. Academic Advice Appointments

Students may request an academic advice appointment when they require more detailed assistance.

Students should provide relevant information before the appointment, such as:

- Programme name.
- Current semester.
- Registered modules.
- Academic concerns.
- Specific questions.

Providing accurate information helps staff provide appropriate guidance.

---

### 11. When to Contact Academic Support

Students should consider contacting Academic Support when:

- They are unsure about a programme requirement.
- They do not understand a registration requirement.
- They have missed an academic deadline.
- They are unsure about changing a module.
- They are concerned about academic progress.
- They need guidance about an internship.
- They are considering deferment or withdrawal.
- They need help identifying the correct university procedure.

---

### 12. Student Responsibility

Academic Support provides guidance, but students remain responsible for completing required procedures and meeting applicable deadlines.

Students should:

1. Check official university information.
2. Identify the relevant procedure.
3. Check applicable deadlines.
4. Ask for assistance when necessary.
5. Submit requests through the approved process.
6. Keep records of important requests and communications.

---

### 13. Summary

The Academic Support Office is a central point of assistance for students who have questions about academic procedures and study planning.

Students should seek support early whenever they are uncertain about a university requirement.
