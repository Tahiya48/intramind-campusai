# Northbridge University
## Academic Calendar and Deadlines

### 1. Overview

The Northbridge University academic calendar contains important dates and deadlines that students need to follow throughout the academic year.

Students are responsible for checking the academic calendar regularly because deadlines may vary between academic periods.

Important academic dates may include registration periods, module adjustment deadlines, examination periods, result publication dates, and semester breaks.

---

### 2. Module Registration Period

The module registration period is the period during which eligible students select modules for an upcoming semester.

Students should complete their module registration during the designated registration period.

The registration period opens and closes according to the dates published in the academic calendar.

Students are encouraged to complete registration before the final day to allow sufficient time to resolve any problems.

---

### 3. Registration Deadline

Students must complete their final module registration before the published registration deadline.

Missing the normal registration deadline may require the student to follow the university's late registration procedure.

Students should not assume that a late registration request will automatically be approved.

Students who expect difficulty completing registration before the deadline should contact the Academic Support Office as early as possible.

---

### 4. Late Registration

A student who misses the normal module registration period may be required to submit a late registration request.

Late registration may involve additional requirements or approval.

The availability of late registration depends on the applicable academic period.

Students should contact the Academic Support Office for guidance if they have missed the registration deadline.

---

### 5. Module Add/Drop Deadline

The university provides a specific period during which students may request permitted changes to their module selection.

These changes may include:

- Adding a module.
- Dropping a module.
- Changing a module where permitted.
- Requesting withdrawal from a module.

Students must submit module adjustment requests before the applicable deadline.

Requests submitted after the deadline may require additional review or may not be accepted.

---

### 6. Module Withdrawal Deadline

The module withdrawal deadline determines the final date on which students may submit a withdrawal request through the applicable university procedure.

Students considering withdrawal should review the consequences of the change and seek academic advice when necessary.

The withdrawal deadline may differ from the module registration deadline.

Students should therefore check the academic calendar for the specific deadline that applies to their academic period.

---

### 7. Examination Period

The academic calendar also identifies the examination period for each semester.

Students should review the examination schedule when it becomes available and ensure that they are aware of the dates and times applicable to their registered modules.

Students should monitor official university announcements for examination-related updates.

---

### 8. Results and Academic Progress

The academic calendar may include the expected period for publication of examination results.

Students should review their results when they become available and check whether any academic action is required.

Students who have questions about their academic progress should contact the appropriate academic support office.

---

### 9. Semester Breaks

The academic calendar identifies semester breaks and other scheduled university holidays.

Students should use the official calendar when planning around semester dates because academic activities may vary between programmes.

---

### 10. Checking Deadlines

Students should use the current Northbridge University academic calendar when checking important dates.

Students should pay particular attention to:

- Module registration opening date.
- Module registration closing date.
- Late registration period.
- Module add/drop deadline.
- Module withdrawal deadline.
- Examination period.
- Results publication period.
- Semester start and end dates.

Students should not rely on dates from previous academic periods because deadlines may change.

---

### 11. Student Responsibility

Students are responsible for keeping track of deadlines that apply to their programme and academic period.

Students should:

1. Check the academic calendar regularly.
2. Record important deadlines.
3. Complete registration before the deadline.
4. Submit module adjustment requests within the permitted period.
5. Check examination dates when they are published.
6. Monitor university announcements for changes.
7. Contact Academic Support if they are unsure about a deadline.

---

### 12. Summary

The academic calendar is the primary reference for important Northbridge University academic dates.

Students should check the calendar before making decisions about:

- Module registration.
- Late registration.
- Adding or dropping modules.
- Module withdrawal.
- Examinations.
- Academic planning.

Deadlines can vary between academic periods, so students should always check the dates applicable to their current semester.
