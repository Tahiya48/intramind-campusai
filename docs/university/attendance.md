# Northbridge University
## Attendance Policy

### 1. Overview

Regular attendance is an important part of successful academic study at Northbridge University.

Students are expected to attend scheduled lectures, tutorials, laboratories, practical sessions, examinations, and other compulsory academic activities associated with their registered modules.

Attendance requirements may vary between programmes and modules.

---

### 2. Attendance Requirements

Students should review the attendance requirements for each module at the beginning of the academic period.

A module may specify:

- Minimum attendance requirements.
- Required practical sessions.
- Compulsory tutorials.
- Laboratory attendance.
- Mandatory presentations.
- Other required academic activities.

Students are responsible for understanding the attendance requirements that apply to their modules.

---

### 3. Recording Attendance

Attendance may be recorded through university systems or by academic staff.

Students should follow the attendance procedures specified by their module or programme.

If attendance is recorded incorrectly, students should report the issue to the relevant academic staff member or academic office.

---

### 4. Absence

Students who cannot attend a required academic activity should inform the relevant academic staff member or university office as soon as possible.

Students should follow the applicable absence procedure.

Depending on the circumstances, students may need to provide supporting documentation.

---

### 5. Unplanned Absence

Students who are unexpectedly unable to attend an academic activity should communicate with the university as soon as reasonably possible.

Students should review any missed learning materials and determine whether additional academic action is required.

Repeated unexplained absence may affect academic progress where attendance is a requirement.

---

### 6. Planned Absence

Students who know in advance that they will be unable to attend a required academic activity should seek approval where required before the absence occurs.

Students should not assume that planned absence is automatically approved.

---

### 7. Attendance and Assessments

Attendance may be connected to assessment requirements for some modules.

Examples may include:

- Practical assessments.
- Laboratory activities.
- Presentations.
- Group activities.
- Participation-based assessments.

Students should check the requirements of each module.

---

### 8. Missing Academic Activities

Students who miss an academic activity are responsible for finding out what content or work they missed.

Students should:

1. Review available learning materials.
2. Check announcements.
3. Contact the lecturer when clarification is required.
4. Complete any permitted missed work.
5. Follow the applicable absence procedure.

---

### 9. Attendance Concerns

Students who are experiencing difficulties maintaining attendance should seek academic support.

The Academic Support Office may help students understand available procedures and identify appropriate support.

Students should seek assistance early rather than waiting until attendance problems significantly affect their studies.

---

### 10. Attendance and Academic Progress

Consistent attendance can help students remain engaged with their modules and maintain academic progress.

Students who frequently miss required activities should review their study plan and seek academic guidance if necessary.

---

### 11. Attendance Records

Students should review attendance records when the university provides access to them.

If a student believes that an attendance record is incorrect, they should contact the appropriate academic staff member and provide relevant information.

---

### 12. Summary

Students should:

1. Understand the attendance requirements of their modules.
2. Attend compulsory academic activities.
3. Follow the university absence procedure.
4. Inform relevant staff about significant absences.
5. Provide documentation when required.
6. Review missed academic material.
7. Check attendance records when available.
8. Seek support if attendance becomes difficult to maintain.
