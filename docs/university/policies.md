# Northbridge University
## Academic Policies and Regulations

### 1. Purpose

Northbridge University's academic policies provide students with guidance about academic responsibilities, programme requirements, module registration, assessment, attendance, and academic conduct.

Students are expected to understand and follow the policies that apply to their programme and academic period.

---

### 2. Student Academic Responsibilities

Students are responsible for managing their academic progress and complying with university requirements.

Students should:

- Understand the requirements of their programme.
- Register for appropriate modules.
- Meet published academic deadlines.
- Attend required academic activities.
- Complete assessments according to the applicable requirements.
- Monitor official university announcements.
- Seek academic advice when they are unsure about a requirement.

Students should not rely solely on information from other students when making important academic decisions.

---

### 3. Programme Requirements

Each programme has specific academic requirements that students must satisfy before graduation.

Programme requirements may include:

- Required modules.
- Elective modules.
- Credit requirements.
- Prerequisites.
- Academic progression requirements.
- Internship or placement requirements where applicable.
- Other programme-specific requirements.

Students should review their programme study plan regularly to ensure that their module selections support their academic progression.

---

### 4. Module Prerequisites

Some modules require students to successfully complete other modules before registration is permitted.

Students should check prerequisite requirements before selecting a module.

If a student does not satisfy a prerequisite, the registration system may prevent the student from registering for the module.

Students who believe that a prerequisite has been incorrectly applied should contact the Academic Support Office.

---

### 5. Module Registration

Students must complete module registration during the designated registration period.

Before submitting their final registration, students should review:

- Their programme requirements.
- Selected modules.
- Module prerequisites.
- Their academic study plan.
- Any registration warnings or restrictions.

Students are responsible for ensuring that their final module selection is appropriate for their programme.

---

### 6. Module Changes

Students may need to change their module selection after initial registration.

Permitted changes may include:

- Adding a module.
- Dropping a module.
- Changing a module where permitted.
- Withdrawing from a module.

Students must follow the applicable module adjustment procedure and submit requests before the relevant deadline.

The availability of a particular module change depends on the academic period and university requirements.

---

### 7. Attendance

Students are expected to attend scheduled academic activities associated with their registered modules.

Attendance requirements may differ between programmes and modules.

Students should review the attendance requirements applicable to each module.

If a student cannot attend an academic activity for a valid reason, they should follow the applicable absence or leave procedure.

---

### 8. Assessment Responsibilities

Students are responsible for completing assessments by the deadlines specified by their programme or module.

Students should:

- Check assessment instructions carefully.
- Record assessment deadlines.
- Submit work through the required system.
- Follow applicable academic integrity requirements.
- Keep copies of submitted work when appropriate.

Students who experience an issue that may affect an assessment should seek guidance from the appropriate academic office as soon as possible.

---

### 9. Academic Integrity

Students are expected to complete academic work honestly and responsibly.

Academic integrity includes:

- Producing original work where required.
- Properly acknowledging sources.
- Following examination rules.
- Avoiding plagiarism.
- Avoiding unauthorized collaboration.
- Not submitting another person's work as one's own.

Students should consult the university's academic integrity guidance if they are unsure whether a particular activity is permitted.

---

### 10. Examinations

Students must follow the examination rules that apply to their programme and registered modules.

Students should check the examination schedule when it is published.

Students are expected to:

- Arrive according to the examination instructions.
- Bring permitted materials only.
- Follow invigilator instructions.
- Avoid unauthorized communication during examinations.
- Follow the university's examination conduct requirements.

Students with questions about examinations should contact the relevant academic office.

---

### 11. Academic Progression

Students are expected to make satisfactory academic progress through their programme.

Academic progression may depend on:

- Successful completion of required modules.
- Credit completion.
- Programme-specific requirements.
- Prerequisite completion.
- Academic performance.

Students who experience academic difficulties should seek advice from the Academic Support Office.

---

### 12. Study Deferment

Students who need to temporarily interrupt their studies may be able to request a study deferment.

A deferment request must follow the university's applicable procedure.

Students should review:

- Eligibility requirements.
- Application deadlines.
- Required documentation.
- The expected period of deferment.
- The effect of deferment on their study plan.

Approval is not automatic and depends on the applicable university requirements.

---

### 13. Programme Withdrawal

Students who decide to permanently leave their programme must follow the programme withdrawal procedure.

Before submitting a withdrawal request, students should consider the academic and administrative consequences of leaving the programme.

Students who are uncertain about their decision should seek academic guidance before submitting the request.

---

### 14. Academic Support

The Academic Support Office provides guidance on academic procedures and requirements.

Students may seek support regarding:

- Module registration.
- Programme requirements.
- Module prerequisites.
- Academic progression.
- Module changes.
- Assessment concerns.
- Examination information.
- Study deferment.
- Programme withdrawal.

Students are encouraged to seek assistance early rather than waiting until an academic deadline has passed.

---

### 15. Official Information

Students should use official Northbridge University information when making academic decisions.

Important information may be communicated through:

- The student information system.
- The university website.
- Official university announcements.
- The academic calendar.
- Academic support staff.

Students should check for updated information because policies, procedures, and deadlines may change between academic periods.

---

### 16. Policy Updates

Academic policies and procedures may be reviewed and updated by the university.

When a policy is updated, students should follow the version that applies to their current academic period.

Students should check official university announcements for significant changes.

---

### 17. Summary

Students are responsible for understanding and following the academic policies that apply to their programme.

In particular, students should:

1. Understand their programme requirements.
2. Check module prerequisites.
3. Complete module registration on time.
4. Review their final module selection.
5. Follow module adjustment procedures.
6. Meet assessment deadlines.
7. Follow examination and academic integrity rules.
8. Monitor their academic progress.
9. Seek academic support when necessary.
10. Check official university information for current requirements.
