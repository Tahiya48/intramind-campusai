# Module Registration

## Online Module Registration

Online module registration allows students to select their modules for the upcoming academic semester.

Students should complete module registration during the designated registration period before the semester begins.

## Reviewing Module Requirements

Before submitting their final registration, students should review their module requirements and make sure that their selected modules are appropriate for their programme and study plan.

## Module Changes

Students who need to add, drop, or withdraw from a module should follow the university's module adjustment procedures.

## Registration Support

Students who have questions about module registration should contact the university's academic support team for assistance.

## Important Information

Registration deadlines may vary depending on the academic period. Students should check the relevant academic calendar and university announcements for important dates.
