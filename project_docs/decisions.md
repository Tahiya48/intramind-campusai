## Decision 01 — Project Direction

### Date

2026-08-17

### Decision

Develop IntraMind CampusAI as an institutional knowledge and intelligence platform for universities. 

### Problem

Crucial univerisity information are always distributed across multiple documents and in various folders, making information retrieval more time-consuming for students and staff. 

### Options Considered

- General-purpose AI chatbot
- Institutional knowledge platform

### Selected Option

Institutional knowledge and intelligence platform.

### Reason

This gives a realistic use case for an organization but also allows the project to demonstrate RAG, natural language processing, machine learning, data analytics, evaluation and software engineering.

### Trade-offs

This project is not just a simple chatbot, but requires document processing, information retrieval, evaluation and analytics.

But this extra complexity provides a more compelling illustration of practical AI engineering and data science skills.
