Module Registration

Online Module Registration

Students can register for modules online during the designated registration period before the beginning of each academic semester.

Before Final Registration

Students should review their programme requirements, module prerequisites, selected modules, and study plan before submitting their final registration.

Module Changes

Students who need to add, drop, or withdraw from a module should follow the university's module adjustment procedures.

Registration Deadline

Students must complete their final module registration before the published registration deadline. Students who miss the deadline should contact Academic Support as soon as possible.

Registration Support

Students who experience registration problems can contact the Academic Support Office for assistance.
