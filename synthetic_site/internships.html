Internships and Work Placements

Overview

An internship allows students to gain practical work experience and apply knowledge gained during their academic studies.

Eligibility

Internship eligibility depends on the student's programme. Students may need to complete prerequisite modules and meet academic requirements before beginning an internship.

Approval

Students should obtain university approval before beginning an internship intended to satisfy a programme requirement.

Required Documents

Students may need to submit an internship offer letter, internship description, employer information, approval forms, and supervisor information.

Internship Assessment

Students may be required to complete an internship report, supervisor evaluation, presentation, logbook, or reflective assignment.

Problems During an Internship

Students experiencing significant problems during an internship should contact their internship coordinator or Academic Support as early as possible.
