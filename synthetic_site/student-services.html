Student Services

Academic Support

Academic Support provides guidance about module registration, programme requirements, academic progression, assessments, examinations, internships, and study planning.

Library Services

The university library provides academic resources, electronic resources, study spaces, computer facilities, borrowing services, and research assistance.

IT Support

IT Support assists students with university accounts, email, learning platforms, campus internet, computer laboratories, and technical problems.

Student Administration

Student Administration supports student records, official documents, administrative requests, and student status information.

Study Facilities

Students can use designated study spaces, laboratories, computer rooms, libraries, and student activity areas according to university rules.

Getting Help

Students should contact the relevant university office when they need assistance and should use current official information when making important academic or administrative decisions.
